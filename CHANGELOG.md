# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **PostPlanify** node:
  - Post: Create, Get, Get Many, Update, Cancel, Delete. Create takes files from previous nodes, URLs or existing media, plus per-platform settings for all 10 platforms.
  - Media: Upload, Get Many, Delete.
  - Comment: Get Many, Get Replies, Reply.
  - Analytics: Workspace Overview, Account Analytics, Account Trends.
  - Workspace and Social Account lookups.
  - Simplify option on post and comment lookups (10 most useful fields).
  - Dates from the date picker use the workflow time zone.
  - Usable as a tool by the n8n AI Agent.
- **PostPlanify Trigger** node: New Scheduled Post, New Published Post and New Comment.
- **PostPlanify API** credential (API key).
